Frequently asked questions about pricing, design, printing and scan tracking.

Pricing & No Subscription

Do I need a subscription?

No, never. You only pay when you do something specific, like changing where a dynamic code points or unlocking a scan stat.

Will my dynamic QR code stop working if I stop paying?

No. There's no monthly plan to lapse, so your dynamic codes keep redirecting. Many other generators switch codes off when a subscription ends; this one doesn't.

Is the static QR code really free?

Yes. You can make and download a static QR code with no account, no sign-up at the end and no fee.

What does a dynamic QR code cost?

Creating one is free. Changing its destination is $25, each scan stat is $5 per code, and each download format for a dynamic code is $5.

Is there a discount code?

Partner referral codes take $25 off your first paid action.

Static vs. Dynamic

What's the difference between a static and a dynamic QR code?

A static code has the link built into the pattern: it's free, but it can't be changed or tracked. A dynamic code sends people through I.Q. Productionz, so you can change the destination later and see scan stats.

Do static QR codes expire?

No. The destination is built into the printed pattern, so a static code works as long as the link it points to works.

Can I change the link after I print the code?

Yes, with a dynamic code. Change the destination in your dashboard for $25, and the printed code stays exactly the same.

Design & Printing

Can I put my logo in the QR code?

Yes. Upload a logo or use a vector logo in your own color, and clear space behind it with a circle, square, diamond, triangle, heart or star cutout. Use the High error correction level when you add a logo.

Can I use my own shape for the dots?

Yes. When you're signed in, upload a one-color SVG and use it as dots, corner dots or both. You can also request a new shape: $5 for one added for everyone, or $50 for one made just for your account. Follow each request in your dashboard.

Will a heavily styled QR code still scan?

The generator warns you about combinations that may not scan, but always test-scan with a phone before you print.

What size and resolution should I use for printing?

Set the size in inches, centimeters, millimeters or pixels and choose 150, 300 or 600 DPI. Quick sizes cover business cards, stickers, flyers, car decals and banners. Use 300 DPI or higher for print.

Scan Tracking

What scan stats can I see?

Total scans, scans today, mobile %, unique scans, scan locations, scans over time, busiest hours and days, devices and browsers, and a scan map. Each stat is $5 per code, and a date range filter is free.

How accurate is scan location?

By default it's approximate, at the city level. You can turn on precise location for a code; the person scanning is asked first and can say no.

Does it work with Google Analytics?

Yes. Turn on the Google Analytics toggle for a code, and scans arrive tagged with UTM values so they show up as QR traffic in your reports.

Can I export my scan data?

Yes. Select your codes in the dashboard for a bulk CSV export ($25) with time, device, browser, language and location for each scan.

Account & App

Do I need an account?

Not for a free static code. Sign in with your email to make dynamic codes, save designs and color palettes, upload shapes and unlock stats.

Can I install the QR Platform as an app?

Yes. On supported phones and computers, an Install App button appears in the menu.
