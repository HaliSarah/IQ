What happens when you stop paying

QR Code Generator PRO's help center says dynamic codes made during its 14-day free trial are deactivated when the trial ends, and scanners land on a service page instead of your content. After a paid plan ends, its codes keep redirecting for 30 days but can't be edited. With IQ QR there's no subscription, so there's no plan to lapse: your codes keep redirecting, and you pay only when you change a destination or unlock a stat.

When QR Code Generator PRO may be the better pick

You edit destinations often, need hosted landing pages, or manage codes as a team. A subscription can cost less than paying $25 per change.

Questions

Will my QR Code Generator PRO code stop working?

Dynamic codes made during its free trial are deactivated when the trial ends, according to its help center. IQ QR codes don't depend on a plan, so there's nothing to expire.

Can I switch my printed codes to IQ QR?

A printed dynamic code always points to its maker's server, so it can't be moved. New codes you make with IQ QR won't depend on a subscription.
