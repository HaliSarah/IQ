AI Video Smoother

Two-pass pipeline for AI-generated video. Pass 1 slows the footage down. Pass 2 fills in the frames so it plays smoothly. One copy — paste into Terminal — walk away.

How To Run
Step 1 — Fill in your folder paths and drop your files below.
Step 2 — Click Copy Commands at the bottom.
Step 3 — Open Terminal on your Mac: press Command + Space, type Terminal, hit Enter.
Step 4 — Paste with Command + V and hit Enter.
Step 5 — Wait. Both passes run automatically one after the other. When you see "All done!" everything is finished.
How it works: Pass 1 fills in frames first (while the video is still at normal speed — frames are closer together so interpolation is cleaner). Pass 2 then slows the clip down to your chosen speed. This order produces significantly smoother results than slowing first.
Pass 1 — Frame Fill

Speed Settings

25% = quarter speed. Lower = slower and smoother but longer processing. 10% is the practical minimum.
Added to filename for Pass 1 output. e.g. clip_120fps.mp4
Pass 2 — Slow Motion

Interpolation Settings

120fps recommended for 25% slow-mo. Higher if going below 25%.
MCI recommended for AI footage.
Lower = better quality, larger file. 18 is visually lossless.
Folder Paths
Source Folder — required
Where your original AI video files are located.
Pass 1 Output — optional
Where slowed files are saved. If left blank, saves to the same folder as your originals.
Final Output — optional
Where the finished smooth files are saved. If left blank, saves to the same folder as your originals.
