🛡️Handling Objections

I can't afford it right now.
I get it — that's exactly why I don't charge monthly. It's one flat payment and you own it forever. I also do a 50/50 split: half to start, half when it's live.
I already have a Facebook page, that's good enough.
Facebook is great for community — but Google can't rank your Facebook page when someone searches for what you do. A website puts you on the map, literally. And you own it — Facebook can take your page down tomorrow.
Can I see examples of your work?
Absolutely — here's my portfolio: [your demo link]. I also offer to build a free homepage mockup so you can see exactly what yours would look like before committing.
What happens if I need changes later?
Minor tweaks in the first 7 days are included. After that I offer a small hourly rate or you can make changes yourself — I'll give you a quick walkthrough so you're never stuck.
72 hours sounds too fast. Will it be quality?
The intake form I send you collects everything I need upfront — your info, photos, colors, content. Once I have that, 72 hours is plenty for a clean, professional 3–5 page site. Quality is my reputation. I'll show you my demo so you can judge for yourself.
