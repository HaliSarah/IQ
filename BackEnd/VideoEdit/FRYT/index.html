Crystal Clear YouTube

Convert any video to YouTube's optimal 2K quality spec — 2560×1440, 24fps, square pixels, high bitrate. Drop files, copy the command, paste into Terminal.

How To Run
Step 1 — Fill in your folder paths and drop your files below.
Step 2 — Click Copy Commands at the bottom.
Step 3 — Open Terminal: press Command + Space, type Terminal, hit Enter.
Step 4 — Paste with Command + V and hit Enter.
Step 5 — Wait. When you see "All done!" your files are ready to upload to YouTube.

Why these specs: YouTube recommends 2K uploads for maximum quality retention after their compression. Square pixels (SAR 1:1) prevent YouTube from re-interpreting non-square pixel sources incorrectly. A 72-frame keyframe distance gives YouTube's encoder clean reference frames for better quality. 40/50Mbps bitrate exceeds YouTube's recommended minimum for 2K, ensuring headroom after their re-encode.
